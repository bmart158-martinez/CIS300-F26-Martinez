BUILD STRENGTH. MASTER MOVEMENT.

Whether you're learning the fundamentals or refining advanced skills, CruxCraft is here to help you become a stronger, more confident climber.

Explore climbing techniques, strength-building exercises, and instructional tutorials designed to improve your performance on the wall.

MASTER THE FUNDAMENTALS

Improving your climbing technique can help you move more efficiently, conserve energy, and approach challenging routes with confidence. Explore these essential climbing skills.

FOOTWORK

Climb with Precision

Good footwork is the foundation of efficient climbing. Learning to place your feet accurately can help reduce unnecessary strain on your arms.

  • Use the toes of your climbing shoes for precise foothold placement.
  • Practice silent feet to improve control and accuracy.
  • Push through your legs instead of relying entirely on your arms.

BODY POSITIONING

Find Your Balance

Understanding body positioning allows you to maintain balance and move more efficiently across different wall angles.

  • Keep your hips close to the wall when it improves your balance.
  • Use flagging to prevent unwanted body rotation.
  • Practice shifting your weight between footholds.

DYNAMIC MOVEMENT

Move with Confidence

Dynamic climbing involves using momentum and coordinated movement to reach holds that may be difficult to access statically.

  • Practice controlled movements before attempting larger jumps.
  • Coordinate your legs, hips, and arms to generate momentum.
  • Develop accuracy when reaching for the next hold.

STRENGTH BEYOND THE WALL

Climbing requires more than grip strength! Building a strong foundation through targeted exercises can improve your stability, control, and overall climbing performance.

CORE STRENGTH

Build Body Tension

A strong core helps you maintain control while climbing steep walls and performing challenging movements.

  • Planks: 3 sets of 30 seconds
  • Dead bugs: 3 sets of 10 reps
  • Hollow body holds: 3 sets of 20 seconds

UPPER BODY STRENGTH

Develop Pulling Power

Upper body training helps develop the strength needed for controlled pulling movements and stability on challenging climbing routes.

  • Assisted pull-ups: 3 sets of 6 reps
  • Resistance band rows: 3 sets of 12 reps
  • Scapular pull-ups: 3 sets of 8 reps

MOBILITY & FLEXIBILITY

Move with Freedom

Mobility training helps climbers move comfortably through different positions and reach challenging footholds more efficiently.

  • Hip mobility drills: 2 minutes
  • Dynamic leg swings: 10 per leg
  • Shoulder mobility circles: 10 reps

Always warm up before training, choose exercises appropriate for your experience, and allow time for recovery between challenging sessions.
