Every climber starts somewhere! Whether you're stepping onto the wall for the first time or working toward your next climbing grade, CruxCraft is here to help you improve.

Explore workouts designed to develop your strength, endurance, technique, and confidence. Take the questionnaire below to find a training routine that matches your experience level and climbing goals.

CHOOSE YOUR TRAINING LEVEL

Every climber has different strengths and goals. Explore the training level that best matches your climbing experience.

BEGINNER

Building the Foundation

New to bouldering? Focus on developing proper climbing technique, balance, footwork, and confidence on the wall.

  • Basic climbing movement and footwork
  • Balance and body positioning
  • General strength and mobility
  • Building climbing endurance

INTERMEDIATE

Developing Your Skills

Ready to take your climbing further? Improve your technique while developing strength, coordination, and endurance.

  • Improving climbing efficiency
  • Core strength and stability
  • Dynamic climbing movements
  • Progressive climbing challenges

ADVANCED

Pushing Your Limits

Experienced climbers can focus on refining difficult movements and developing more specialized strength.

  • Advanced movement techniques
  • Power and explosive movement
  • Climbing-specific strength
  • Projecting challenging boulders
